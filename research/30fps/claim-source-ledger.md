# Claim-Source Ledger: Full-Resolution 30 FPS

Access date for web sources: 8 September 2026.

| ID | Claim | Source and evidence | Confidence | Contradiction or gap |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stock Wyze Cam v4 is limited to 20 fps day and 15 fps night at 2560x1440. | [Wyze official specifications](https://support.wyze.com/hc/en-us/articles/23746258989851-Wyze-Cam-v4-Tech-Specs), video section. | Very high | Does not identify which subsystem imposes the limit. |
| 2 | Current supported RTSP setup has no frame-rate control. | [Wyze RTSP instructions](https://support.wyze.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026245231-Wyze-Cam-RTSP) and [v4 beta announcement](https://forums.wyze.com/t/wyze-cam-v4-firmware-beta-test-7-27-2026/344026/1). | High | Public UI documentation may not enumerate private engineering controls. |
| 3 | WYZEC4 was certified with SC401AI, OS/OV04D10 and GC4653 sensor alternatives. | [FCC internal photographs](https://fccid.io/2AUIUWYZEC4/Internal-Photos/FCC-Interal-Photos-Rev01-7194677.pdf). | Very high | An individual camera's fitted sensor must still be identified. |
| 4 | Each certified sensor family supports full-size output near 2560x1440 at 30 fps. | [OS04D10 brief](https://www.omnivision-group.com/files/pd/file/OS04D10-PB-v1.1-WEB.pdf), [GC4653 data sheet](https://dl.sipeed.com/fileList/MaixIII/AXera/07_Chip_manual/GC4653_CSP_datasheet%C2%A0for%C2%A0release_Rev.1.0_20190712.pdf), [SC401AI flyer](https://tehno32.ru/sites/default/files/download/cmos_smartsens/sc401ai_v2_0.pdf). | High | Sensor capacity does not prove complete Wyze pipeline capacity. |
| 5 | Wyze v4 uses an Ingenic T41NQ with 128 MiB DRAM. | [Observed boot log](https://github.com/gtxaspec/wz_mini_hacks/issues/760). | High | Community-captured log rather than a Wyze bill of materials. |
| 6 | T41 includes H.264/H.265 hardware encoding and advertises up to 4K20. | [Ingenic T41 product page](https://www.ingenic.com.cn/products-detail/id-33.html). | Very high | QHD30 feasibility inferred from pixel rate still needs profiling. |
| 7 | Genuine 30 fps requires camera firmware/sensor changes; a Pi cannot create missing captured information. | Deduction from claims 1-6 and sampling theory. | Very high | A future Wyze firmware could change the conclusion. |
| 8 | FFmpeg can export supported H.264 decoder motion vectors as frame side data. | [FFmpeg extract_mvs example](https://ffmpeg.org/doxygen/trunk/extract__mvs_8c_source.html), `flags2=+export_mvs` and `AV_FRAME_DATA_MOTION_VECTORS`. | Very high | Codec vectors are sparse prediction vectors, not dense optical flow. |
| 9 | The tested live Wyze main stream is 20 fps, I/P-only, two-second GOP, with motion-vector side data on each observed P-frame. | Direct 4-second `ffprobe -flags2 +export_mvs` measurement on the Pi 5 test host, 8 September 2026. | High for tested camera/time | Other cameras, firmware and low-light modes must be audited. |
| 10 | FFmpeg can duplicate, blend or motion-compensate frames to a target FPS. | [FFmpeg minterpolate documentation](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#minterpolate). | Very high | Documentation makes no Raspberry Pi performance promise. |
| 11 | CPU MCI misses real time even at 640x360 on the tested Pi 5. | Direct 10-second test: 0.548x speed, approximately 16 output fps, 18.328 s CPU, 126 MB peak RSS. | High for tested settings | Other search settings may trade quality for speed; none prove 1440p30. |
| 12 | Temporal blend sustains 30 at 640x360 and clears 30 for one 2560x1440 focused stream on the tested Pi 5. | Direct tests: 360p at 1.11x live speed and 0.476 s CPU; 1440p at about 34 processing fps, 1.39x, 6.326 s CPU and 188 MB peak RSS. | High for tested settings | A 30-minute 1440p soak and four-stream tests remain required. |
| 13 | ANVIL reuses H.264 motion vectors and leaves a convolution-dominated INT8 residual graph. | [ANVIL preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26835), abstract and paper. | Medium-high | Recent preprint; Snapdragon results do not establish Pi/Hailo performance. |
| 14 | Stock RIFE is not a direct Hailo solution because dynamic warping/GridSample is unsuitable or unsupported. | [Hailo support discussion](https://community.hailo.ai/t/support-for-gridsample-operation/13957) and ANVIL analysis. | Medium-high | Custom graph partitioning can work, but transfer overhead needs measurement. |
| 15 | Pi 5 exposes Vulkan 1.3 and AI HAT options; AI HAT+ 2 reaches 40 TOPS INT4. | [Pi 5 specification](https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/raspberry-pi-5/) and [AI HAT documentation](https://www.raspberrypi.com/documentation/accessories/ai-hat-plus.html). | Very high | TOPS is not an interpolation benchmark. |
| 16 | Pi 5 lacks an H.264 hardware encoder, so direct display avoids costly re-encoding. | Raspberry Pi, [H.264 encoding performance white paper](https://pip-assets.raspberrypi.com/categories/685-app-notes-guides-whitepapers/documents/RP-010033-WP-1-H.264%20encoding%20performance%20on%20Raspberry%20Pi%205_series%20computers.pdf). | High | Software encoding is viable for some single-stream formats. |
| 17 | Interpolation can produce severe artifacts at occlusions, discontinuous motion and lighting changes. | [Frame Interpolation Transformer and Uncertainty Guidance](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/papers/Plack_Frame_Interpolation_Transformer_and_Uncertainty_Guidance_CVPR_2023_paper.pdf) and [WACV 2025 robust VFI paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2025/papers/Park_Explicit_Guidance_for_Robust_Video_Frame_Interpolation_against_Discontinuous_Motions_WACV_2025_paper.pdf). | High | Security-camera-specific failure rates require a local dataset. |
| 18 | Worldwide novelty is not established. | Prior art in claim 13 plus established codec-vector and display-warp techniques. | Very high | A formal patent search and independent review are outside this engineering study. |
